About

Analytical depth, public-health purpose.

My work sits at the intersection of statistical modeling, epidemiologic thinking, and data storytelling.

I am pursuing a Master of Public Health at Universitas Airlangga with a focus on Biostatistics and Health Data Science. My projects range from clinical-risk prediction and health-service segmentation to spatial disease modeling and workforce-distribution analysis.

I approach every project by clarifying the decision problem, protecting data privacy, checking assumptions, reporting uncertainty, and translating results into language that researchers, practitioners, and decision-makers can use.
